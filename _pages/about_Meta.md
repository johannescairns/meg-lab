**Alumni**

**Xhilda Meta, MSc**

Xhilda worked as a Project Assistant at the MEG Lab from August to October 2026. She supported experiments on microbial evolution and antibiotic response, focusing on establishing and optimising an in vitro model for urinary tract infections. She holds an MSc in biotechnology from Lund University and has experience in synthetic microbial co-cultures and clinical microbiology. Her interests include microbial community dynamics, antibiotic resistance, and developing solutions for infections caused by resistant pathogens.
