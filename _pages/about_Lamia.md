**MSc Researcher**

**Lamia Said, BSc**

I am a Master’s student in Molecular Biology at Lund University, specialising in Microbiology and Biotechnology. My Master’s thesis in the MEG Lab focuses on the evolution of antibiotic resistance and biofilm formation in a polymicrobial catheter-associated urinary tract infection model. Antibiotic resistance is one of the major challenges in microbiology, and I am very interested in understanding the processes that contribute to it. I have a particular interest in the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying microbial processes, as well as how microorganisms interact and coexist. I am therefore drawn to experimental research that explores how microorganisms function, adapt and interact.
