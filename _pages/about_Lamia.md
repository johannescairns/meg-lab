**MSc Researcher**

**Lamia Said, BSc**

I am a Master’s student in Molecular Biology at Lund University, specialising in Microbiology and Biotechnology. As an MSc Researcher at MEG Lab, I am investigating the evolution of antibiotic resistance and biofilm formation in a polymicrobial model of catheter-associated urinary tract infection. My current focus is on understanding how microbial interactions influence adaptation and resistance within these communities. I am particularly interested in the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying microbial processes, microbial community dynamics, and how microorganisms adapt, coexist and respond to antibiotic treatment.
