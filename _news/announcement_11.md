New funding: Our lab has received SEK 1,000,000 from the Crafoord Foundation for the project [CompESBL](https://www.crafoord.se/bidrag/beviljade-bidrag/vetenskaplig-forskning/), which investigates competitive dynamics and horizontal transfer of antibiotic resistance plasmids between ESBL-producing and commensal *Escherichia coli*.
