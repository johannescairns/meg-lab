Johannes Cairns discusses controlled evolution experiments, data-driven approaches to life science, the predictability and control of evolution, and strategies to tackle antimicrobial resistance. The conversation also covers upcoming research on polymicrobial urinary tract infections. The interview is part of the [AMR Podcast Series with Surajit Pal](https://youtu.be/ljtdUNdRi1M), hosted by the Kiel University Graduate Center.
